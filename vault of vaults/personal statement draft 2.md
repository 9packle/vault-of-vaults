I would like to study this course because I have always been interested in the sciences since starting formally in year 3, and gradually I came to especially enjoy chemistry since it can explain everything from an atomic level, to macroscopic observations, such as the structure and behaviour of materials or the mechanisms by which vital processes in our bodies occur, all through both theory dictated by and experimentation.

My subject choices at school have always been focused on maths and the sciences, and my A level choices have further enriched my understanding of Chemistry: Maths and Further Maths have given me the tools to approach difficult questions; Physics has given me additional context on many ideas and theories in Chemistry as well as an appreciation for the connection between the three subjects; Chemistry has taught me the fundamentals concepts and theories importance of experimentation to confirm theory, as well as piquing additional interest in chemistry outside of curriculum.
These subject choices also tie into my achievements in competitions, which I actively looked for as a way to stretch my understanding of the subjects, both in the paper itself and in the preparation process for the competition which stretched my knowledge of the subjects. I achieved a Gold in the Senior Maths Challenge twice and subsequently a Merit in the British Maths Olympiad both times. The SMC both forced me to look more for a fast and optimal way to solve the questions as well as be accurate under time pressure, which I worked on by looking at a problem and trying to find as many solutions as possible. The Olympiad was different again and required me to learn to rigorously structure solutions while ruling out all other possibilities so that by logic the answer provided was the only one. This way of thinking also helped for some organic synthesis questions where I initially wasn't able to work out the next molecule in the step, as I could use a similar way of thinking to eliminate possible answers with the given information until the answer revealed itself.
My interest in Chemistry also led me to achieve a Gold with a score of 55 in the Round 1 of the UK Chemistry Olympiad as well as a Gold twice in C3L6. Throughout the preparation my favourite questions were the organic synthesis questions, since initially they were hard to even approach them knowing very little of the common reagents and reaction pathways, but I slowly went through common reagents and types of reactions to be able to work through each step of synthesis pathways. Each piece of information I picked up felt rewarding since often through looking at one type of mechanism, many other scenarios with similar functional groups could also be solved by following the same train of thought.
